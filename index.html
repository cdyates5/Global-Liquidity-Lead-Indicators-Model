Global Liquidity Lead Indicator Model

A leading-indicator model for global liquidity growth, built on public data. Nine market and macro indicators are weighted by how well they have led liquidity growth over the past ten years. Their sum, less liquidity's own z-score, signals whether liquidity growth should rise or fall over the medium term.

Global liquidity is measured as central bank balance sheets + broad money + FX reserves ex gold, for the economies with timely public data, in USD.
